  A comprehensive note of legal observations presented at the hearing by Gianfranco Schiavone and
  Giovanna Cavallo (Forum/Road Map on the EU Pact), incorporating the points previously raised by
  Paolo Bonetti. It challenges the abuse of emergency legislation in breach of Article 77 of the
  Constitution — given the two years granted for implementation and the parliamentary examination
  already under way on A.S. 1869 — the absence of any consultation, and the widening of the scope of
  administrative discretion from which automatisms arise that are incompatible with the fair
  individual assessment of applications, invoking the doctrine of counter-limits.
