  Analyses the amendments approved by the European Parliament on 10 February 2026 to the Asylum
  Procedure Regulation (Regulation 2024/1348), subsequently adopted as Regulations (EU) 2026/463 and
  2026/464: the introduction of the common EU list of safe countries of origin and the new
  definition of safe third country, applicable immediately and not from 12 June 2026. A precise
  political and legal choice, the author argues, which entrusts presumption with an ever more
  central function in the European asylum system, restricting access to the right and compressing
  judicial protection.
