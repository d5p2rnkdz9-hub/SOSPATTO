sommario: >-
  Advance publication of paragraph 1.2.1 of Part II of the Manuale del diritto di asilo (Pacini),
  updated to the Pact and Decree-Law 100/2026. The editor reconstructs the transition to “a common
  procedure” under Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 and highlights its two lines of development: on the one
  hand the new guarantees — free legal counselling entrusted to a person rather than to information
  material, independent monitoring, the non-derogable nature of the interview, identification of
  vulnerabilities even during the procedure or before the court —, on the other the multiplication
  of special procedures. It falls to the judiciary to keep guarantees and constraints in balance,
  also by relying on the continuing case law of the Court of Justice on Article 47 of the Charter.
