  Reproduces the memorandum filed for the hearing of 25 June 2026 before the Senate Constitutional
  Affairs and Justice Committees on Bill no. 1939 converting Decree-Law 100/2026. It examines
  screening, detention for checks and detention; the procedural guarantees and the legal fiction of
  non-entry in border procedures; appeals, implicit withdrawal and acceleration; Eurodac,
  interoperability and data protection; the organisation of the judiciary and the clearing of the
  backlog; the limits of the financial neutrality clause; and the scope for Parliament to intervene
  during conversion.
