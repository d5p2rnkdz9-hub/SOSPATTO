  Memorandum of the Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore of Milan on the constitutional lawfulness
  of the decree, both with regard to the constraints of the Constitution and to the international
  obligations that operate as a benchmark under Articles 10 and 117 of the Constitution. It starts
  from the abuse of the power to legislate by emergency decree in breach of Article 77 of the
  Constitution, noting that the regulations and the directive of the Pact expressly provided two
  years for implementation and that the bill approved by the Council of Ministers on 11 February
  2026 (A.S. 1869) has in practice been set aside.
