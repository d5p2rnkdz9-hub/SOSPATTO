sommario: >-
  Second part of the thematic report of the Office of the Massimario on Decree-Law 100/2026 converted
  by Law 145/2026 (the first, Report No 35 of 25.06.2026, was limited to the appeal in cassation
  and to the Court's jurisdiction over the new measures). The report reconstructs the whole
  framework of the domestic implementation of the EU Pact, read also in the light of the first decisions
  of the specialised divisions and of the Court of Cassation: authorisation to reside in a
  specific place and judicial complaint, the new “risk of absconding”, detention under Articles 6 and 6-bis of
  Legislative Decree 142/2015, alternative measures, the three stages of making an application, accelerated and
  border procedures, manifest unfoundedness, subsequent applications, the right to remain and
  authorisation to remain, the new procedure under Articles 35-bis and 35-ter of Legislative Decree 25/2008, amended
  Article 32 of the Consolidated Immigration Act, implicit withdrawal, amendments to the Consolidated Immigration Act linked to Regulation (EU) 2024/1356
  (screening, apprehension, detention for screening purposes), the residual court-support office (ufficio per il processo)
  (also in light of Decree-Law 144/2026) and the transitional regime. The key to the reading, confirmed
  in the opening, is that the Government “appears to have favoured the most restrictive solutions
  among those compatible with the legal framework deriving from European Union law”.
