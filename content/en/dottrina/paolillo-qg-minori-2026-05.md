sommario: >-
  The Public Prosecutor at the Juvenile Court of Genoa examines how the Pact and the Italian
  implementing bill transform the legal condition of unaccompanied foreign minors: from the shift of
  competence over assisted return from the juvenile judicial authority to the administrative
  authority of the Prefect, to the risks of treatment contrary to Article 3 ECHR in mixed placements
  with adults. The protection of the child, the author argues, cannot be curtailed in the name of
  managing flows, operating as a genuine constitutional and international counter-limit.
