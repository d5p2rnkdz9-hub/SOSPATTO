  Observations of the University of Rome Tor Vergata on the alignment of the legal system with
  Regulations (EU) 2024/1346, 1348, 1351, 1356 and 1358. It quantifies the burden imposed on Italy —
  over 16,032 applications to be examined at the border in the first year, the highest share in the
  Union, amounting to 26.7% of the total, and up to 24,048 applications between 13 June and 14
  October 2027 — and criticises both the recourse to a decree-law despite two years for adjustment
  and the heterogeneity of a text that combines immigration with the digitalisation of justice and
  the reform of the bar examination.
