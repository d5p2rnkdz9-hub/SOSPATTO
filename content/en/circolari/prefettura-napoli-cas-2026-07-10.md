Note from the *Prefettura* of Naples (the State's territorial office) — Area IV Immigration, replying to the request to activate
reception measures for a Bangladeshi national who arrived in Italy on 4 June 2026 via
the Balkan route, and who expressed the wish to apply for international protection to the Immigration
Office and the Territorial Commission of Naples by certified email, through counsel, about a month
after arrival.

Before proceeding to the assessments within its remit, the Prefettura asks for further
information to be sent and, in particular:

- the **date of actual entry** of the applicant into national territory;
- if he expressed the wish to apply for international protection **more than ninety
  days** after entering Italy, any elements useful to show **good cause for the
  delay**, under Article 1(2-*bis*) of Legislative Decree 142/2015;
- any documentation attesting to **conditions of vulnerability** relevant under
  Article 17 of Legislative Decree 142/2015.

The note is significant as practice because it shows the first use, at the stage of access to
reception, of the ninety-day time filter introduced into Legislative Decree 142/2015: a criterion that
operates upstream of the assessment of reception measures and may lend itself to investigative
delay. From the defence standpoint, what matters is the immediate allegation of any
vulnerability profiles and the communication of the date of entry together with a request for access to reception.
