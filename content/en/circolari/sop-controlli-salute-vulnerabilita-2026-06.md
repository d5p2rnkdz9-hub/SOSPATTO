The **standard operating procedures (SOPs)** define the arrangements for preliminary
health checks and for the assessment of **vulnerabilities** during the screening
of third-country nationals at the external borders, in implementation of Regulation (EU) 2024/1356.
The document is accompanied by the adopting circular of 12/06/2026 and by eight technical
annexes: health forms, vulnerability checklist, information sheet and indicators on
trafficking, list of anti-trafficking contacts and referral schemes (Frontex, USMAF-SASN).
