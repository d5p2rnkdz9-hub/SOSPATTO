Note from the *Questura* of Bologna (police headquarters) — Immigration Office, signed by the Head V.Q.A. Dr Claudia
Storto — addressed to the AUSL, the Municipality, the Revenue Agency and the Chamber of Commerce of
Bologna. It serves an operational liaison function: it explains to those who will
interact daily with the applicant which document he or she will present from now on, in place of the
residence permit for asylum application, and with what effects on access to rights.

## The new document: the «Certificate of expression of the wish to apply»

The communication starts from the legal premise — Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 applicable from 12 June 2026 and
Decree-Law 100/2026 adapting the national legal order — and notes the practical consequence: at the
end of registration at the Immigration Office «the issue of the temporary residence
permit for asylum application is no longer provided for». The foreign national is issued «solely a Certificate of
Expression of the Wish to Apply (Article 29(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348; Article 4(1) of Legislative Decree 142/2015), on which
a photograph of the applicant and his or her tax code will be affixed (where generated
by the system)». Where the applicant is accompanied by minor children, the Certificate also shows their
photograph with personal details and tax code.

## The role of the Certificate in the transitional regime

The substantive point lies in the coordination with Italian law. The note recalls that, «although
the Certificate bears the wording according to which within 21 days the applicant must proceed to
lodge the application with the Territorial Commission», during the transitional regime
in force until 31 October 2026 «registration of the application takes the place of lodging». It
follows that «the Certificate of expression of the wish to apply will be the only document in fact available to the
applicant for international protection, until the outcome of the proceedings». It is the operational reverse of the
Italian choice to postpone lodging before the Commissions: the only document in circulation
is the one issued at the time of registration.

## The recipient administrations and the applicant's rights

The rationale of the communication is the same as that of the clarifications already circulated in other Questure: to ensure
that the Certificate is accepted as a valid title for the exercise of the rights granted to the applicant for
protection by the legislation in force. The note recalls four specific references: registration with the
National Health Service (Article 21(1) of Legislative Decree 142/2015); education of minors (Article 21(2));
access to work after ninety days from the application (Article 22(1)); registration in the
population register (Article 5-bis(1)). The institutional interlocutors (AUSL, municipal registry office, Revenue
Agency for the tax code, Chamber of Commerce for registration in the Business Register)
are advised that the Certificate is the only documentation with which, at this stage, the holder will be able to
claim the corresponding benefits.

## Practical usefulness for the defence

The note is a useful documentary confirmation, which can be relied on in a complaint or a brief, when the local
administration objects to the applicant's lack of a paper residence permit or denies him or her
a benefit for insufficiency of title. The Questura of Bologna expressly describes the Certificate of
expression of the wish to apply as the «only document in fact» of the applicant during the transitional regime, and
traces access to rights back to the national legislation already in force. It should be read together with the
certificate of the Questura of Trento published on this site, which shows the model of the document
handed to the applicant.
