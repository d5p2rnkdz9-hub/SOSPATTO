Circular of the Department for Civil Liberties and Immigration which, in implementation of the
European Pact on Migration and Asylum, notifies the European Commission (pursuant to
Article 54(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348) of the **identification of the border zones** in
which the procedures are to be carried out. The circular stresses the national geographical
connotation that excludes no site a priori, also providing for **mobile hotspots**, and updates the
overview of «adequate capacity» (8,016 places): 3,132 in government first-reception
centres, 3,804 in reception, 3,519 in operational hotspots, and the centres of Modica,
Porto Empedocle and Gjader (489 places) designated for **detention**.
