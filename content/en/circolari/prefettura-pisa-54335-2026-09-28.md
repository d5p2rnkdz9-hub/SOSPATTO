The *Prefettura* of Pisa, by circular of 28 September 2026, transmits to the local bodies the operational
guidance of the Ministry of the Interior contained in circular no. 45722 of 21 September
2026 on access to services by applicants holding the new documentation under
Article 4 of Legislative Decree 142/2015. It follows the line already traced by the
[note of the Questura of Bologna of 13 July 2026](/en/circolari/questura-bologna-attestato-2026-07-13.html)
and by the
[circular of the Prefettura of Varese no. 93 of 22 September 2026](/en/circolari/prefettura-varese-93-2026-09-22.html).

## The applicant's new documentation

Article 10 of Decree-Law 100/2026 has “entirely replaced” Article 4 of Legislative Decree 142/2015. When
the application is registered, the Questura or the Border Police Office issues to the applicant a
“personal document, bearing the unique identity code assigned following the fingerprinting activities,
the photograph of the holder and the personal details declared by the foreign national during those
same activities”; the document “certifies that the application has been registered” and
“allows the holder to be recognised under Article 1(1), point (c), of Presidential
Decree No 445 of 28 December 2000”. The new rules “supersede the previous system based on the issue of the
residence permit for asylum application”.

## The old residence permit can no longer be required

The Prefect asks the offices to recognise and accept the new documentation in place of the previous
residence permit for asylum application: “failure to produce the previous residence permit for asylum
application cannot, in itself, constitute an obstacle to access to the services, benefits and administrative
procedures to which the applicant is entitled under the legislation in force, where the person concerned holds
the documentation issued under new Article 4 of Legislative Decree No 142 of 2015”.

## Adapting practices and IT procedures

The Ministry recalls “the need to avoid administrative practices or IT procedures
still based on the previous rules causing undue obstacles or delays
in access to the benefits and services recognised by the legal order”. Any further requirements provided for by the
specific sector legislation remain unaffected.

## Practical usefulness for the defence

The circular confirms, from a second Prefecture, that setting the previous residence permit against the applicant has no
basis under the new rules. Ministerial circular 45722/2026, which it reproduces in full, remains for the time being not public.
