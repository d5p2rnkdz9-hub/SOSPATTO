Reply from the *Questura* of Livorno — Immigration Office to a certified email in which a lawyer asked
for an appointment for access to the asylum procedure. The note describes the operational arrangements
in force in the provincial capital and in the province:

- those interested in expressing the wish to apply for asylum **may appear every week, from
  7:00 a.m., on Monday and Thursday, in front of the Caserma Labate** (Viale Boccaccio no. 5), «to start
  the procedures relating to fingerprinting»; only «subsequently will the foreign national be given an
  appointment in order to lodge his or her asylum application»;
- applicants resident in the province may express the wish at the competent
  **detached police Commissariati** (Rosignano, Cecina, Piombino, Portoferraio),
  «exclusively on Monday starting from 8:00 a.m.», with the same procedure;
- as to the possibility of requesting the appointment for lodging **by certified email**, the
  Questura «reiterates that this option is reserved exclusively for those belonging to the so-called
  “vulnerable categories”, in accordance with the legislation and the Departmental Circulars».

The document is useful because it captures the most fragile point of the new procedural sequence: the
gap, even of weeks, between the expression of the wish and the formal registration of the
application, with the trail of consequences in terms of access to reception and rights. See, for the
same area, the [monitoring of the disembarkation at the port of Livorno on 14 June
2026](/en/circolari/monitoraggio-sbarco-livorno-2026-06-14.html).
