Framework agreement between the **Department for Civil Liberties and Immigration** of the
Ministry of the Interior and the **Italian Red Cross Association** for activities
connected with the reception of migrants following arrivals on national
territory, adopted within the framework of the EU Pact regulations (RAMM, procedures, screening,
crisis) and of the Consolidated Immigration Act. The agreement refers to Article 32 of Decree-Law
23/2026, which allows the Ministry to avail itself of the Italian Red Cross until 31 December 2028.

**Annex 1 — Response plan for screening procedures** of the Italian Red Cross National
Committee is available as an attachment. It governs the composition of the roving
teams, the activation procedure and the procedure for the health assessment, the scope of
operations, logistics and costs, the provision of goods and services, staff organisation,
the Red Cross coordination unit and monitoring.
