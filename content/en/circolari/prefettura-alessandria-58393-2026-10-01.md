The *Prefettura* of Alessandria, by circular of 1 October 2026 signed by Deputy Vice-Prefect
Eugenio Licata, transmits to the local bodies the operational guidance of the Ministry of
the Interior contained in circular no. 45722 of 21 September 2026, in the same terms as the
[note of the Questura of Bologna of 13 July 2026](/en/circolari/questura-bologna-attestato-2026-07-13.html),
the [circular of the Prefettura of Varese of 22 September 2026](/en/circolari/prefettura-varese-93-2026-09-22.html)
and the [circular of the Prefettura of Pisa of 28 September 2026](/en/circolari/prefettura-pisa-54335-2026-09-28.html).

## The applicant's new document

Article 10 of Decree-Law 100/2026 has “entirely replaced” Article 4 of Legislative Decree 142/2015: when
the application is registered, the Questura or the Border Police Office issues to the
applicant a “personal document” bearing the unique identity code (C.U.I.), the photograph of the
holder and the declared personal details, which “certifies that the application has been registered” and “allows the holder to be
recognised under Article 1(1), point (c), of Presidential Decree No 445 of 28 December 2000”. The new rules
“supersede the previous system based on the issue of the residence permit for
asylum application”.

## The old documentation can no longer be required

The circular states that the new documentation “is fully suitable to certify the status of the applicant for
international protection, and the person concerned cannot be required to hold the previous residence permit for
asylum application”. It follows that “failure to produce the previous residence permit for asylum application
cannot, in itself, constitute an obstacle to access to the services, benefits and administrative
procedures to which the applicant is entitled under the legislation in force”.

## Adapting practices, procedures and IT tools

The addressee bodies are “invited to ensure the recognition and acceptance of the
documentation provided for by amended Article 4 of Legislative Decree 142/2015, adapting, where necessary,
the administrative procedures, operating methods and IT tools”. Any further requirements provided for by the
specific sector legislation remain unaffected.

## Practical usefulness for the defence

The value of the circular for the defence is one of consolidation: a third Prefecture (after Varese and
Pisa) applies ministerial guidance 45722/21.09.2026 — not yet public — in the same
terms. Setting the previous residence permit against the applicant is expressly
excluded as a cause of delay in access to services, benefits and administrative procedures.
