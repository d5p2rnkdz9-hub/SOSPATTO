For the Pact, only Article 4 is relevant: it amends Article 17 of Decree-Law 100/2026 (converted
without amendments by Law 145/2026). The decree has been in force since 30 September 2026 and must be
converted into law by 28 November 2026.

## The amendments to Article 17 of Decree-Law 100/2026

- **Paragraph 1**: the transitional competence of police headquarters (Questure) and border police
  offices to receive the making, registration and lodging of the application, for all applicants,
  is extended from 31 October 2026 to **30 April 2027**. Paragraph 2 is unchanged: until then the
  application is deemed lodged at the time of registration.
- **Paragraph 3**: the nominative document issued upon registration under Article 4(1) of
  Legislative Decree 142/2015 **is equivalent to the document referred to in paragraph 4** of the
  same Article 4. It is valid for **one year from the date of registration** of the application,
  without prejudice to the renewal provided for in Article 29(9) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348, and
  allows the holder to work under Article 22(1) of Legislative Decree 142/2015, that is, ninety days
  after the application was lodged if the procedure has not been concluded and the delay cannot be
  attributed to the applicant.

## The other provisions

Article 1 extends until 31 January 2027 the contracts of the staff of the trial office (1,350 units,
including the immigration backlog office); Articles 2, 3 and 5 concern the traineeship of ordinary
magistrates, the powers of the National Anti-Mafia Prosecutor in proceedings against minors, and the
steel plants of strategic national interest.
