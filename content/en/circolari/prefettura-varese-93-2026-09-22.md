The *Prefettura* of Varese, by circular no. 93 of 22 September 2026 signed by Deputy Prefect
Federica Crupi, transmits to Mayors, ATS Insubria, ASST Sette Laghi and Valle Olona, the Territorial
Labour Inspectorate, INPS and the Revenue Agency the operational guidance of the Ministry of
the Interior — Central Directorate for Civil Services for Immigration and Asylum, contained in
circular no. 45722 of 21 September 2026, on access to services by applicants
holding the new documentation under Article 4 of Legislative Decree 142/2015. The subject is the same as that of the
[note of the Questura of Bologna of 13 July 2026](/en/circolari/questura-bologna-attestato-2026-07-13.html),
here addressed on the basis of ministerial guidance.

## The new documentation replaces the old residence permit for asylum application

Decree-Law 100/2026 (Article 10) has «entirely replaced» Article 4 of Legislative Decree 142/2015: when
the application is registered, the Questura or the Border Police Office issues to the
applicant a «personal document, bearing the unique identity code assigned following the
fingerprinting activities, the photograph of the holder and the personal details declared by the foreign national
during those same activities». The document «certifies that the application for
international protection has been registered and allows the holder to be recognised under Article 1(1),
point (c), of Presidential Decree No 445 of 28 December 2000». The
new rules «supersede the previous system based on the issue of the residence permit for
asylum application».

## The reminder to local authorities: the old documentation can no longer be required

The Ministry takes note of the difficulties reported by holders of the new documentation
in accessing services and benefits, attributable «to the lack of knowledge of the new legal
framework on the part of the offices and bodies called upon to provide those services (Municipalities, ASLs, etc.)».
The circular states that «failure to produce the previous residence permit for asylum
application cannot, in itself, constitute an obstacle to access to the services, benefits and
administrative procedures to which the applicant is entitled under the legislation in force,
where the person concerned holds the documentation issued under new Article
4 of Legislative Decree No 142 of 2015».

## Adapting practices and IT procedures

The Ministry recalls «the need to avoid administrative practices or IT procedures
still based on the previous rules causing undue obstacles or delays
in access to the benefits and services recognised by the legal order», and invites the offices to
«adapt the administrative procedures and operating methods adopted». Any further
requirements provided for by the specific sector legislation remain unaffected.

## Practical usefulness for the defence

The circular can be relied on in a complaint or a brief when municipal, health or
social security administrations set against the applicant the lack of the previous residence permit. It reports the
content of ministerial circular no. 45722 of 21 September 2026, the text of which is not
available here.
