Circular of the National Commission for the Right of Asylum which, supplementing
guidance note no. 4842 of 12/05/2026, gives the Territorial Commissions
**first operational guidance** on the decree-law containing urgent provisions for the
first implementation of the European Pact on Migration and Asylum: access to the procedure,
the **transitional rules** between the old and the new regime, follow-up steps and operational
coordination with the border procedure sections.
