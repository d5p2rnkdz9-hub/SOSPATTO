List of the nationalities for which the **percentage of first-instance decisions granting
international protection is 20% or lower**. This is the statistical datum
that triggers the ground for accelerated examination laid down in Article 42(1), point (j), of Regulation (EU)
2024/1348 and, through Article 45, the mandatory border procedure: it is therefore
the operational document to which the “determinations” of the Territorial Commissions
ordering the special procedures refer.

The document specifies that the rate is calculated as the ratio between recognition
decisions (refugee status or subsidiary protection) and the total number of first-instance
decisions, that the reference aggregate is the **EU excluding Denmark** and that
the aggregate was compiled **exclusively for the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348**, in order to
determine eligibility for the accelerated examination procedure under Article 42(1),
point (j). The data were extracted from the online database on **21 May 2026** and
refer to the year 2025; the document warns that the data published online may
differ because of subsequent corrections and revisions transmitted by national providers.

The non-absolute nature of the presumption is moreover written into point (j) itself, which
preserves the cases in which the determining authority assesses that the situation in the third country has
undergone a significant change, or that the applicant belongs to **a category of
persons whose protection needs make it impossible to regard as representative** a
rate of 20% or lower, also taking into account the significant differences between
first-instance and final decisions — aspects on which, according to the [Court of
Bologna](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-10979-2026-07-23.html), the burden of allegation and proof
lies with the determining authority.
