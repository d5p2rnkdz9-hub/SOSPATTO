The Tribunal of Venice (judge Alessia Capriuoli) finds that the conditions for the accelerated border
procedure are not met, converts the procedure from Article 35-*ter* of Legislative Decree 25/2008 to
Article 35-*bis* of the same decree, declares the applicant's right to remain in the territory and orders the issue of the
document under Article 4(4) of Legislative Decree 142/2015, with automatic cessation of every imposed
measure. The decision of the *Commissione territoriale* of Padua of 4 September 2026 had been taken in the border
procedure under Article 43(1), point (a), and Article 42(1), point (j), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348, the applicant being a
Pakistani national whose application was registered in Agrigento on 18 August 2026.

## The scope of the interim review

The decree opens with an explicit position on its scope: the interim stage is “the natural forum” for
reviewing the correct activation of the border procedure and compliance with the information obligations, since the decision
affects the applicant's legal position and “future destiny in life”. The extremely short time for preparing a defence prevents the
applicant from being burdened with pleading requirements: under Article 35-*ter*(3) and (4) of Legislative Decree 25/2008, it is
the administration that must supply the “complete body of knowledge” already at the interim stage.

## Timely information as a precondition of Article 42, point (j)

The information form was handed over “many days after” registration, in breach of Articles 8(2) and 15(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348. The omission is not merely
formal: it renders the statements “not fully informed”, so that the statistical criterion of Article 42(1), point (j), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 — which requires
“comparison with the personal history” and not the abstract datum alone — lacks the necessary anchor. The Tribunal
places the breach within the “constructive dialogue” between authority and individual (CJEU, 14 May 2020, C-924/19 PPU and C-925/19 PPU).

## Supervening vulnerability and the duty of review

In the presidential determination the applicant is classified as a potential victim of trafficking and as
subject to serious psychological, physical and sexual violence. The reference to the reception centre's “medical and
psychological support” is “absolutely apodictic” and is not proved by any document; the hearing record shows an Arabic-language
interpreter, a language foreign to the applicant, and the applicant alleged visible wounds suffered in Libya, with supporting documentation.
Articles 21(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 and 23(1) of Directive (EU) 2024/1346 require “constant review” of the participatory guarantees
and of the accelerated procedural choices when vulnerability emerges in the course of the procedure: a condition disregarded here, also in
light of the post-traumatic stress disorder mentioned in Article 24 of the Reception Conditions Directive.

## The intertemporal regime of the Ministerial Decree of 21 July 2026

The application was registered on 18 August 2026, when the Ministerial Decree of 21 July 2026 had not yet entered into force:
the communication provided for by Article 54 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348, to which the Ministerial Decree is linked, “is by no means merely formal, but rests on a careful and weighed assessment of the
places equipped with all the adequate structures and competences”. The transfer of persons to places not designated under Article 54 “before the entry into force of the Ministerial Decree
automatically gives rise to full authorisation to enter the national territory” (referring to
Cass. no. 18531/2026 on the intertemporal relevance of the expression of the wish to apply). The
decision follows the line already traced by
[Trib. Venice 13622](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-venezia-13622-2026-09-22.html) and
[13498](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-venezia-13498-2026-09-22.html) on defects in the information provided and on the
20% threshold, adding a novel passage on the duty to reassess the special guarantees owed to vulnerability in the course of the procedure.
