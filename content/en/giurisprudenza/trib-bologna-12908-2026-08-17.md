The Tribunal of Bologna (judge Angela Baraldi) declared automatically suspended the rejection by which the *Commissione
territoriale* of Bologna — Forlì-Cesena Section, applying Regulation (EU) 2024/1348, had rejected as manifestly
unfounded under Article 39(4) the application of a Bangladeshi national. The decree predates by sixteen days
[Trib. Bologna 13590/2026 of 4 September](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-13590-2026-09-04.html) — same judge, same
argument, different proceeding — and precedes it in giving reasons for the reading of Article 17 of Decree-Law 100/2026
on which the summary of 4 September dwells.

## The pivot: «presentation» in paragraph 4 is not «lodging» in paragraph 2

There are three dates: expression of the wish — coinciding with entry into reception — on 19 May 2026; lodging of the
application on 24 June; decision of the Commission on 10 July. The Commission applied the new Regulation «on the
assumption, it must be supposed, of lodging at a date after its entry into force».

The pivot is a comparison within the Italian transitional rule itself. Paragraph 2 of Article 17 of Decree-Law 100/2026
governs the moment at which the application is to be regarded as **lodged**; paragraph 4 makes the retention of the
former rules conditional on **presentation** before 12 June 2026. Two different words in the same provision rule out
that they are synonyms: had the legislator wanted equivalence, it would have used the same term, conforming to the
vocabulary of Article 79(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348.

## Placement in reception as a documented expression of the wish

Placement in the CAS is, under the former rules, a «significant documentary element of the expression of the wish
already made»: under Article 2 of Legislative Decree 142/2015 applicant status followed from the expression of the wish
alone, and in the present case a PEC from the centre to the *Prefettura*, dated 19 May 2026, attests to it. Directive
2013/32/EU and the implementing rules required lodging within three working days of the expression of the wish — six in
border procedures, extendable by ten for «exceptional circumstances» to be spelled out. None of these time limits was
complied with, and the delay is not attributable to the appellant.

## Procedural consequence

The former rules being applied, failure to comply with the accelerated procedure downgrades it to an ordinary one (Joint
Chambers no. 11399/2024): the Commission could not have ruled manifest unfoundedness, the effect of the rejection is
automatically suspended and the time limit for the appeal returns to thirty days.
