The Tribunal of Bologna (judge Stefania Cannavale) treats the request for suspensive effect as a
request for a finding of the right to remain, declares Article 35-bis of Legislative Decree 25/2008
applicable instead of Article 35-ter of Legislative Decree 25/2008 and finds that the contested
decision is automatically suspended by the appeal. The *Commissione territoriale* of Bologna had
rejected the application as manifestly unfounded under Article 39(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348,
considering that the case fell under Article 42(1), point (j), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348.
According to information provided by the defence and not ascertainable from the text of the decree,
the applicant is an Algerian national who landed in Cagliari and was transferred to the border
procedure centre in Bologna (former Gamberini barracks).

## Review of the 20% threshold

The regularity of the procedure is reviewed of the court's own motion; the Administration filed no
observations. The Tribunal finds that the reference to point (j) in the President's determination
“remains merely assertive and is confined to the reference to the Eurostat statistical figure”,
and that the rejection decision is reasoned in the same way. The authority must state in concrete
terms why the applicant does not belong to categories exposed to specific risks and why the
percentage is significant in the case at hand, engaging with the elements already on file.

## Effects on the procedure

Absent that assessment, the border procedure “cannot be regarded as validly initiated”: the
non-automatic nature of suspension, the shortened time frame of the proceedings and the finding of
manifest unfoundedness under Article 28-ter of Legislative Decree 25/2008 all fall away. The decree
excludes the cases of Article 68(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 and, recalling judgment no.
11399/2024 of the Joint Chambers (Sezioni Unite) of the Court of Cassation, extends the principle
from breaches of time limits to breaches of procedural guarantees.

## Place in the case law

The reasoning follows that of [Tribunal of Palermo 9739/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-9739-2026-07-25.html),
including the reference to Article 42(3), point (e), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348, which concerns
unaccompanied minors: for an adult applicant the relevant condition is Article 42(1), point (j),
alone. On the same threshold in the border procedure, see
[Tribunal of Venice 13498/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-venezia-13498-2026-09-22.html) and
[Tribunal of Venice 13922/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-venezia-13922-2026-09-30.html); taking a
narrower view where the *Commissione* gives several concurrent grounds,
[Tribunal of Florence 11088/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-firenze-11088-2026-09-11.html). The
decree examines no other issues; according to the defence, the appeal also alleged breaches of the
information obligations and of free legal counselling.
