The Tribunal of Bologna, International Protection Division (judge Marco
Gattuso), ruling on the application under Article 700 of the Code of Civil Procedure lodged together with the
complaint under Article 5-*quinquies* of Legislative Decree 142/2015 against the decision of the Prefect of
Bologna **authorising residence in a specific place** of an applicant for international
protection (a Pakistani national) subject to the accelerated border procedure under Articles 42(1), point (j), and 43 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348, has
partially granted the request for an order *inaudita altera parte*, referring to
the precedent of the Tribunal of Palermo of 27 June 2026 on the admissibility of the atypical remedy under
Article 700 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

The decree **clarifies the scope of the constraints** that may be imposed on the applicant: the only
lawful requirement, absent broader written indications from the
*Prefettura*, is to sign every day the attendance register kept by the managing
body, any restriction on freedom of movement as to time or territory having to be ruled out
(any assimilation to a measure restricting personal liberty under Article 13 of the Constitution is therefore excluded); it also recognises the lawyer's right to
meet the client at the place of residence as well. The Tribunal also points out,
with a view to the hearing on the merits, the burden of proof borne by the
determining authority as to the regularity of the accelerated procedure and the Eurostat statistical data
on the country of origin (referring to Cass. No. 6745/2021 and Cass. Joint Chambers 11399/2024 on the
ex officio nature of judicial review of special procedures). The text of the
decision is published in redacted form to protect personal data.
