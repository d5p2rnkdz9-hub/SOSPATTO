The Tribunal of Turin (judge Alessandra Aragno) declares that there is no need to rule
on the interim request — the enforceability of the contested measure being “already suspended by
operation of law” as a result of the acknowledged reinstatement of the ordinary procedure — and, for the sake of completeness,
authorises the appellant to remain in the territory of the State pending the decision on the
merits. The appellant, from a safe country, had been served with a decree of rejection by the
Commissione territoriale following an accelerated procedure, but the actual timing of the
administrative proceedings proved to be out of line with that provided for by law.

## The timing: lodging and decision

The Tribunal notes that the application was lodged on 30 July 2026 and the
Commission's decision was taken on 18 August 2026: a period of time exceeding the horizon of the
accelerated procedure, whose time limits were not “respected in its procedural
stages”.

## The principle of the Joint Chambers 11399/2024 applied to the new regime

From the case law of the Court of Cassation the Tribunal derives — “as a principle of law also applicable
to the present case, Court of Cassation, Joint Chambers, no. 11399/24, filed 29.4.2024” — the rule that the
derogation from the general principle of automatic suspension of the contested act of the Commissione
territoriale, in respect of a person from a safe country, operates “only where
the Commissione territoriale has applied a correct accelerated procedure”. “In the opposite case, where the accelerated procedure has not been respected in its
procedural stages, the ordinary procedure is reinstated and the general
principle of automatic suspension of the Commissione territoriale's measure applies anew”.

## The procedural outcome and the link with the Pact

The procedure for assessing the application “must be brought within the ordinary one
provided for by Article 27 of Legislative Decree 25/2008”, with the consequent application of Article 35-bis(2),
first sentence, and (3), first sentence. Accordingly the suspension of enforceability “is to be
understood as automatic” and the Tribunal declares that there is no need to rule on the request, while
simultaneously authorising the appellant to remain.

The decree belongs to the line of case law extending the principle of the Court of Cassation sitting in Joint Chambers to the
new regime of the Pact. It is linked to
[Trib. Trieste 3940 (D'Alessio)](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-trieste-3940-2026-07-31.html), which — by a
different route — likewise concluded in favour of automatic suspension, and runs parallel to the reading of
the Florence
([11088 (Castagnini)](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-firenze-11088-2026-09-11.html)) and Milan
([36319 (Masetti Zannini)](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-milano-36319-2026-09-19.html)) sections on the failure to
respect the time limits of the accelerated procedure.
