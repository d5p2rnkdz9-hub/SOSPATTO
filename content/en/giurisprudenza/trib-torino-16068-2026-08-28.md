The Tribunal of Turin (judge Valerio Brecciaroli) declares that there is no need to rule on the interim request — the
enforceability of the contested measure being «suspended *ex lege*» as a result of the recognised restoration of the
ordinary procedure — and refers the case file to the designated judge for the setting of the merits hearing. The
appellant, a Peruvian national, had expressed the wish to apply on 25 May 2026, but the *Questura* had arranged for the
application to be lodged (C3 form) only on 21 July 2026, more than a month after the entry into force of Decree-Law
100/2026. The *Commissione territoriale* had applied Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 and Decree-Law 100/2026 to its decision, on
the premise that the application had been lodged after 12 June 2026.

## New procedural rules, former substantive rules

The decree grants the principal request for suspension *ex lege* by giving weight to the date of the expression of the
wish rather than to that of the C3. Article 17(4) of Decree-Law 100/2026 refers to the «presentation» of the application:
in light of the recent case law of the Court of Cassation (Cass. Ord. 18.5.2026, no. 14851), an asylum application,
«although lacking the typical forms of lodging, is capable of producing its effects where it is addressed to an authority
attributable to the State apparatus and displays a minimum degree of functional suitability to set the procedure in
motion»; the expression of the wish, even if not formalised, «operates as the criterion for identifying the rules
applicable over time, determining the moment of origin of the application».

## The time elapsed between expression of the wish and C3

In the present case the expression of the wish is documented «at least on 25.05.2026» (exhibit 6 of the appeal), whereas
the lodging took place on 21.07.2026, «well beyond the time limit of three working days (or six in the case of an
expression of the wish to apply for international protection to the border police) laid down in Article 26(2-*bis*) of
Legislative Decree 25/08». The case is therefore brought back to the ordinary procedure of Article 27 of Legislative
Decree 25/2008, with the consequent application of Article 35-*bis*(2), first sentence, and (3), first sentence, and the
automatic suspension of the enforceability of the contested measure.

## Alignment with the line of case law

The decree confirms, four days later and with a different judge, the reading of
[Trib. Torino 16082/2026 (Alessandria)](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-torino-16082-2026-08-24.html): the expression of the wish
is the act that anchors the applicable rules, and the *Questura*'s delay in lodging is not neutral. It is also linked to
[Trib. Milano 31896 (Minieri)](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-milano-31896-2026-09-29.html), which a month later carries the
same reading over to Legislative Decree 25/2008 and disapplies Egypt as a safe country of origin.
