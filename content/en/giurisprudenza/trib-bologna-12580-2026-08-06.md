The Tribunal of Bologna (judge Cristina Reggiani) grants *inaudita altera parte* the interim application of an applicant
in the border procedure and authorises him to reside not in the centre assigned by the Prefect but with his wife, who is
lawfully resident and close to giving birth.

On 29 July 2026, at 16.39, counsel had asked the *Prefettura* to authorise the applicant — a Moroccan national just
fingerprinted — to reside with his wife rather than in the CAS of destination. At 17.25 the Prefect signs the
authorisation to reside exclusively in the CAS, with no reference to the PEC and no reasoning on the implicit refusal; to
the subsequent request for variation the *Prefettura* replies by sending back the same order in a different layout.

## The lack of reasoning of the Prefect's order

Atypical interim protection being admitted in the complaint under Article 5-*quinquies* of Legislative Decree 142/2015,
which provides the remedy but «does not contemplate specific typical interim measures capable of immediately suspending
the effects of the Prefect's order» (so already
[Trib. Palermo, 27 June 2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-8695-2026-07-25.html)), and the aspects relating to the
border procedure determination being reserved for the merits:

> NOTING that, in adopting the order referred to in Article 5-ter of Legislative Decree 142/2015, the Prefect must carry
> out a case-by-case assessment in compliance with the principle of proportionality of the measure and must account for
> the criteria applied with reasoning in fact and in law;
> CONSIDERING that in the present case such reasoning is entirely lacking […]

The documentation produced proves «a very particular personal situation»; the choice of exclusive place of residence
infringes «his fundamental right to family unity», and the Administration has not shown «particular needs corresponding
to overriding interests» that would justify it.

## The legal fiction of non-entry does not require the centre

The premise of the border procedure — that «the applicant's physical presence does not amount to legal entry into the
national territory» — is «in any event preserved» by the authorisation to reside at the wife's address, «a place suited
to guarantee his prompt availability». The applicant has no further constraints «either as to hours or as to territory»:
the Prefect's order contained no ancillary requirements, and the interim judge adds none.

## The Administration's burdens for the hearing

The hearing having been set in written proceedings, the decree lists what the determining authority will have to file.
The verification of the correctness of the special procedures is ex officio (Court of Cassation no. 6745/2021; Joint
Chambers no. 11399/2024). It falls to the Administration to document the checks on vulnerabilities; to adduce the
Eurostat statistics relating to the country of origin, including as regards forms of protection other than refugee
status and subsidiary protection; to prove that the person does not fall within a category for which the percentage is
not representative, since «unlike in the concept of safe country of origin — there is no presumption that shifts a
burden of allegation onto the asylum applicant»; and finally to produce the registered and lodged application.

On the merely indicative value of the Eurostat datum see
[Trib. Palermo, R.G. 9739](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-9739-2026-07-25.html); on the individual nature of the
authorisation to reside,
[Trib. Bologna, 23 July 2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-10979-2026-07-23.html).

> **Editorial note.** In the passage on the Eurostat statistics the country of origin is given as «Pakistan», whereas
> the appellant is a Moroccan national: in all likelihood a slip, but it is in the text.
