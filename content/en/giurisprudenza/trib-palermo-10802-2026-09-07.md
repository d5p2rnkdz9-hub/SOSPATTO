The Tribunale di Palermo, holiday-period section (judge Dr Flavia Coppola), revokes the decision by
which the Prefect of Agrigento, on 23 July 2026, had authorised a Tunisian national in the accelerated
border procedure to reside exclusively in the Sikania Centre in Siculiana for twelve weeks. The decree of
7 August had set the hearing and refused suspension *inaudita altera parte*; at the hearing the
complainant was heard, and the Administration did not appear.

## Timeliness: the service report does not prove the information

The Administration raised an objection of lateness: the decision had been delivered on 23 July with an
Arabic interpreter, and the complaint filed on 5 August. The Tribunal rejects the objection. The
documentation "does not make it possible to verify what the actual content of the communication was":
the record attests in a generic, pre-printed formula that the recipient understood, without saying
whether the act was translated in full, whether the means and time limits for challenge and the
consequences of non-compliance with the requirements were explained, "which are not even indicated
therein". Physical delivery having been proved but not compliance with the information obligations of
Article 8 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 and Article 9(5) of Directive (EU) 2024/1346, actual knowledge coincides with the day on which the
complainant turned to counsel, 1 August: the complaint is timely.

## The merits: information on the consequences is not a mere formality

Article 9(5) of Directive (EU) 2024/1346 — referred to by Article 54 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 — requires the applicant to be
informed in writing of the decision, of the means of challenging it and "above all" of the
consequences of non-compliance, in a language he or she understands and by a concise and accessible
communication: it is a "necessary condition for the applicant to be able to conform his or her conduct
knowingly". The decision was drawn up only in Italian, does not appear to have been translated in full
and does not indicate the consequences of breaching the obligation to reside; serious consequences,
because non-compliance may amount to failure to cooperate and lead, in the cases of Article 41 of
Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 and Article 23-*bis* of Legislative Decree 25/2008, to implicit withdrawal of the application. The
breach is therefore not a formal one and the decision is revoked, the other grounds being absorbed —
nullity of the presidential order, restriction of liberty *sine titulo*, failure to activate independent
monitoring. The question of the constitutionality of Article 5-*ter* for conflict with Article 13 of the Constitution
is declared irrelevant and in any event unfounded: the measure, by its concrete content, is not a
deprivation of personal liberty. Costs offset owing to the novelty of the issues.

The decision is in line with [Trib. Palermo 8694](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-8694-2026-07-25.html) and
[8695](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-8695-2026-07-25.html) on the information obligations in the
Sikania line of cases; it adds the criterion of actual knowledge for the purposes of the time limit for the complaint.
