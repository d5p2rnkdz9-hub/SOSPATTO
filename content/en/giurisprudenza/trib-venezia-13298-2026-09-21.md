Two decrees of the Tribunal of Venice (judge Tobia Aceto), issued one day apart in
different cases but with the same *ratio decidendi*, declare suspended *ex lege* the rejections as
manifestly unfounded by the Commissione territoriale and find that the appellants have the right to
remain in the territory. Both procedures had been applied to applications lodged after
12 June 2026, and the Commission had applied Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 and Legislative Decree 25/2008 as
amended by Decree-Law 100/2026.

## A determination that does not “channel” into the border procedure

The defect is common to both: the determination of the President of the Commission of 21 August 2026 “ordered
exclusively the application of the accelerated procedure under Article 42 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348”, whereas the
final decision “also applied the border procedure without any corresponding channelling
measure appearing”. The finding goes beyond the individual case: the border procedure is a
special regime with respect to the accelerated procedure, and its application requires a
formal act that “channels” the application into it, a generic reference in the decision not being
sufficient.

## The duty of reassessment under Articles 20, 21 and 53 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348

In the first case (R.G. 13298-1/2026, decree of 21 September 2026) “in the course of the
personal interview, elements emerged that are potentially relevant for the special procedural
needs and vulnerability of the applicant, connected with reported gender-based violence and
a situation of serious psychological suffering, without the Commission having carried out the
reassessment prescribed by Articles 20, 21 and 53 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348”. In the second case (R.G.
13384-1/2026, decree of 22 September 2026) the element was in fact already crystallised: in the
screening form of 14 August 2026 the applicant had been classified as a “person
subjected to abuse/neglect by parents/other relatives” and the special reception and special procedural needs — access to mental health care, assignment of an interpreter
trained in communicating with vulnerable applicants, interpreter of the preferred sex — had
been expressly identified. Despite this, the determination of 21 August stated that “from the
available documentation no signs of vulnerability emerged”, proceeding to apply
the accelerated procedure; further elements of psychological suffering that emerged at the interview did not
trigger the reassessment.

## The procedural effect

The twofold unlawfulness — misalignment between determination and decision, failure to reassess
vulnerabilities — causes the conditions of Article 68(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 to cease to exist, with the
re-expansion of automatic suspension under Article 35-*bis*(3) of Legislative Decree 25/2008. The
further complaints and any question on the merits are absorbed.

On the point of the genericity of a determination that repeats the statutory wording without fitting it to the
specific case, see also
[Trib. Milano 19 September 2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-milano-36319-2026-09-19.html).
