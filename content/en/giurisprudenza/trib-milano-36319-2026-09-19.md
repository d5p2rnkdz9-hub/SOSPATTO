The Tribunal of Milan (judge Elena Masetti Zannini) records that the conditions for the accelerated border procedure are
not met, converts the procedure from Article 35-*ter* to Article 35-*bis* of Legislative Decree 25/2008 and declares
automatically suspended the rejection for manifest unfoundedness by the *Commissione territoriale* of Milan. The
applicant had expressed the wish at the screening in Agrigento on 3 August 2026, registered the application on 5 August
and had been transferred on 7 August to the CAS Mancini among the migrants «pre-channelled into the PAF»; the President's
determination is of the same 7 August.

## The place of the procedure before the Ministerial Decree of 21 July 2026

Milan is relevant not as the place where the application was made (Article 43(1), points (a) to (d), of Regulation (EU)
2024/1348), but as a «place for carrying out the asylum procedure at the border» within the meaning of Article 54(3) of
the Regulation. The province was identified only by the Ministerial Decree of 21 July 2026, which entered into force on
25 August; the Ministerial Decree of 5 August 2019 did not include it. Since the procedure begins with the expression of
the wish (Article 26 of the Regulation; Court of Cassation, 15 September 2026, no. 25359), it took place «from its
start, in a place that had not yet been identified»: the administration «in fact authorised the appellant's entry into
the national territory», with the consequence that the procedure is ordinary.

## «Apprehension in connection with the crossing» in the strict sense

The determination based the procedure on Article 43(1), point (b), of the Regulation. In the light of recital 57 and of
the case-law of the Court of Justice on Article 2(2)(a) of Directive 2008/115/EC (CJEU, C-47/15, *Affum*, para. 72;
CJEU, Grand Chamber, 19 March 2019, *Arib*, para. 46), the situation presupposes a «close temporal and spatial link» with
the crossing: border procedures «should not be applied automatically to persons apprehended inland».

The determination instead merely repeats the statutory wording, and nothing is reasoned as to the place of apprehension.
The notification of the screening at Lampedusa suggests rather a disembarkation after a search and rescue operation, a
situation different from point (c). The vagueness of the reasoning on the condition «infringes the procedural safeguards
by which these procedures are bound, preventing adequate judicial review».

## The consequences

The procedure becomes that of Article 35-*bis* and the derogation from the suspensive effect in Article 68(3)(a)(ii) of
the Regulation no longer applies: the measure remains suspended for the whole duration of the proceedings. The same judge
decided in the same terms on the apprehension the following day
([Trib. Milano 36532-1/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-milano-36532-2026-09-20.html)). On the place of the procedure
before the Ministerial Decree see also
[Trib. Venezia 13622-1/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-venezia-13622-2026-09-22.html).
