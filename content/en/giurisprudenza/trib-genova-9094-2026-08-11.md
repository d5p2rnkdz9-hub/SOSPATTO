The Tribunal of Genoa (judge Andrea Perelli) orders the Questura of La Spezia, *inaudita
altera parte*, to issue immediately a residence permit for asylum application or another document
attesting the expression of the wish to apply for protection, stating the date of the
expression, of registration or lodging and any element useful for
identification. The applicant, a Bangladeshi national, had expressed, by certified e-mail from his lawyer on 23
April 2026, the wish to submit a subsequent application; the appointment had been fixed for 24
June; on that occasion the Questura had refused a copy of the C3 form and of the receipt, without
replying to the further request of 5 August; on 4 August the rejection of the
application had been notified.

## The applicable rules are fixed at the expression of the wish

The decree starts from Article 17(4) of Decree-Law 100/2026, read with Article 2(1)(a) of Legislative Decree
142/2015: an applicant is also a person who «has expressed the wish to apply for that protection», so that the
administrative procedure «begins when the applicant expresses his wish
to apply for that protection, regardless of the moment at which the application was
lodged». Begun on 23 April 2026, the procedure remains subject to the former rules. This is
the same reading as [Trib. Naples 17179](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-napoli-17179-2026-08-04.html) and
[Trib. Bologna 13590](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-13590-2026-09-04.html), here applied not to the
suspension of the refusal but to the document owed to the applicant.

## The document owed and the harm caused by its absence

The *fumus* has four elements: from the expression of the wish the foreigner is an applicant (recital
27 of Directive 2013/32/EU); he is entitled to the permit for asylum application or at least to a receipt which,
once the time limit for lodging has expired — thirteen working days under Article 26(2-*bis*) of Legislative Decree
25/2008, counting the extension for the high number of applications — serves as a
provisional permit under Article 4(3) of Legislative Decree 142/2015; he is entitled to reception (Article 17 of Directive
2013/33/EU; Article 1(2) of Legislative Decree 142/2015); the procedure is not concluded, the time limit for
challenging the rejection still running. The *periculum* lies in the absence of a document attesting the regularity of his presence:
arrest and deprivation of liberty, expulsion, inability to work lawfully and to access
reception, the population register and the health service. The same risk of immediate removal justifies the
decree without summoning the other party under Article 669-*sexies*(2) of the Code of Civil Procedure; the confirmation hearing
is set for 19 August 2026.

The decree is the first, among those published, to address the Questura's refusal to document the
application; it should be read alongside the cases of the attestations of
[Trento](/en/circolari/attestazione-manifestazione-trento-2026-06.html) and of
[Livorno](/en/circolari/questura-livorno-appuntamenti-2026-07-08.html).
