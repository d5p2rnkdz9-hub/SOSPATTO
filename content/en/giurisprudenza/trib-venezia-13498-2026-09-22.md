The Tribunal of Venice (judge Alessia Capriuoli) finds that the Egyptian national appellant has the right
to remain in the national territory for the duration of the proceedings, as a
consequence of the automatic suspensive effect of the appeal arising from the unlawfulness of the
special procedure followed, and orders the issue of the document under Article 4(4) of Legislative Decree
142/2015. The Commissione territoriale of Verona had rejected the application as manifestly unfounded, at the
session of 7 August 2026, within an accelerated procedure activated under Article 42(1),
point (*j*), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348.

## The unticked information box is a systemic procedural defect

The document produced in the proceedings shows that “no information specifically
concerning the rights under Articles 15 et seq. of the Procedures Regulation” was provided, “the
relevant box not being ticked”. The omission “points to a serious procedural defect that bears on the full
awareness of the statements to which not only the final decision, but the very choice of
procedure, must constantly relate”: scrutiny of the information leaves the realm of the
remedies additional to the decision on the merits and is situated “upstream”, at the level of the very classification of the
procedure.

## The 20% threshold and the specific claim

The Commission says nothing about the actual representativeness of the 20% recognition-rate
parameter “in relation to the specific claim put forward by the appellant, which concerns
conscientious objection exercised in relation to military service”. The necessary comparison with the
specific claim — which “must take place at every stage of the procedure” — means that the statistical parameter
cannot automatically be transposed: in the light of the qualified sources (IRB —
Immigration and Refugee Board of Canada; OFPRA, *Égypte: Le service militaire obligatoire*,
January 2025), “the risk of torture and the death penalty” is envisaged for the Egyptian conscientious objector. The loss of consistency of the parameter of Article 42(1), point (*j*) renders the
accelerated procedure “without anchor” and requires reversion to the ordinary procedure.

## The procedural outcome

The Tribunal finds the right to remain “as a consequence of the automatic suspensive effect of the
appeal arising from the unlawfulness of the special procedure followed, the case falling within the scope
of the ordinary procedure” and orders the immediate issue of the document under Article 4(4) of Legislative Decree
142/2015. Costs are offset on account of the novelty of the questions and, in particular, of the application
of very recently introduced European legislation.

The decree builds on the line of authority already opened by
[Trib. Roma 39909](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-roma-39909-2026-09-12.html) and
[Trib. Palermo 9739](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-9739-2026-07-25.html) in refusing
the automatism of the statistical parameter, adding the element of the unticked box as a significant
symptom of the information defect and an analytical development on the sources on Egyptian conscientious
objection.
