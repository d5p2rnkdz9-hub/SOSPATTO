The Tribunale di Milano (judge Elena Masetti Zannini) does not validate the detention at the CPR of
via Corelli ordered by the Questore of Varese on 30 July 2026, under Article 6(2), points (*c*) and
(*d*), of Legislative Decree 142/2015, of an Algerian national whose application for protection had
been pending before the Commissione of Brescia since November 2025. On the evening before the hearing
the detainee had been arrested at the CPR for aggravated damage to property, and the *Questura*
declared it impossible to bring him back in time for a hearing within forty-eight hours.

## The presence of the detainee admits of no exceptions

The first ground is procedural. Article 6(5) of Legislative Decree 142/2015 “provides for the presence
of the detainee at the validation hearing as necessary, albeit ‘remotely by audiovisual link’”; the
words “where possible” relate to the link and allow the judge to go to the CPR, not to dispense with
the hearing. As these are rules concerning personal liberty, “to be interpreted strictly”, the Code
of Criminal Procedure on deferring the appearance of an arrested person for legitimate impediment
does not apply by analogy. Since the detainee could not be heard, “it cannot be said that the
proceedings were correctly instituted, on pain of breach of his right of defence”; the presence of
counsel is irrelevant, because there remains “the person's right to be heard in a free interview at the
(civil) validation hearing”. For this reason alone validation is refused.

## The merits, for completeness

On point (*c*) the Questore indicated “convictions” for theft and numerous criminal reports: the
fingerprint records show identifications and arrests, including under aliases, but nothing from which
a conviction can be inferred; the criminal record, requested from the Public Prosecutor's Office on
1 August and chased several times, had not arrived at the time of the decision. Dangerousness must be
“current, serious and concrete” and convictions have no automatic relevance (Constitutional Court
no. 202/2013): the condition is not met. On point (*d*), the decree repeats the approach of
[Trib. Milano 28137](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-milano-28137-2026-07-06.html): two cumulative conditions,
a risk of absconding to be assessed case by case under Article 5-*sexies* of Legislative Decree
142/2015 and the need to determine elements of the application that cannot be obtained without
detention. Here the missed hearing prevents individual assessment and the Questore does not indicate
those elements, although the application is pending before the Commissione; Cass. no. 21523/2026 is
cited.
