The Tribunal of Turin (judge Fabrizio Alessandria) has declared that there is **no need to rule** on the request for
authorisation to remain «since the enforceability of the contested measure is already **suspended *ex lege***». To reach
that result the judge must establish that the accelerated procedure had been unlawfully adopted, and does so by giving
weight to the date of the expression of the wish rather than to that of the C3 form.

## New procedural rules, former substantive rules

> **holding** that, to applications for international protection expressed on a date before 12.6.2026, although the new
> procedural rules introduced by Decree-Law 100/2026 apply, the **substantive rules in force before** the entry into force
> of the EU Pact on Migration and Asylum continue to apply.

The participle is that one: applications **expressed** before 12 June, not registered or lodged.

## Proof of the date of the expression of the wish

The wish had been expressed on 11 May 2026, and the proof is the document issued by the Italian Red Cross of Turin,
«that is to say by one of the "*associations that handle the booking and scheduling of the expression of the request for
international protection on the prenotafacile platform*"», according to the list of authorised desks published by the
*Questura*. The certificate of the contracted body is not a party's document, but the trace of a step organised by the
Administration itself, which Decree-Law 100/2026 has moreover brought inside the procedure ([Article
26-*ter*](/patto-interattivo/dlgs-25-2008/index.html#art_26-ter) of Legislative Decree 25/2008).

## The delay in lodging

The time elapsed between the expression of the wish and the drawing up of the C3 is not irrelevant «also because the
Administration, from the very first moment, has had the possibility of assessing the existence, in the abstract, of the
conditions for the possible start of the accelerated procedure (for example in the case of origin from a safe country of
origin; so literally **Trib. Firenze, decree of 18.4.2025**)». If the condition could be assessed from the first contact,
the delay in lodging cannot turn into an advantage for the one who caused it. The Florence precedent predates the Pact and
is here reused as a criterion of transitional law.

## The procedural consequence

With the accelerated procedure gone, the procedure «must be brought back within the **ordinary** one provided for by
Article 27 of Legislative Decree 25/2008», with application of Article 35-*bis*(2) and (3), and suspension of enforceability
«**automatic** (see again **Cass. Sez. Un. no. 11399/24**)». The interim request is not rejected but declared superfluous;
the proceedings continue in the ordinary forms, with the hearing set for 20 April 2032, and the suspension *ex lege*
covers their entire course.

This is the mirror-image situation to that of [Turin, 11 August](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-torino-2026-08-11.html), by the
same judge. The criterion was subsequently circumscribed by
[Trib. Bologna, 4 September 2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-13590-2026-09-04.html), which makes the relevance of
the expression of the wish conditional on the delay being attributable to the Administration.

The decree does not indicate which provision of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 the Commission had invoked for the accelerated
examination, nor does it rule on the appellant's vulnerability, alleged among the grounds of appeal according to the
defence: the issue remains absorbed and unprejudiced.
