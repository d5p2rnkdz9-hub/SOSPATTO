The Tribunal of Bologna (judge Silvia Rossi) declares automatically suspended the decision
of the *Commissione territoriale* of Bologna that had rejected the application as manifestly unfounded in
the accelerated procedure under Article 28-*bis*(2), point (c), and (1), point (b), of Legislative Decree 25/2008. The defect is
a missing document: the determination of the President on the type of procedure — the basis for
the choice of acceleration — does not appear to be signed.

## The President's determination as logical antecedent

As to the time limits of the administrative procedure, the decree finds no consequences: «the time limits of the
accelerated procedure under Article 28-bis appear to have been complied with». The point lies elsewhere. The
Commission, although invited to make submissions on the point, did not overcome the objection:

> despite the invitation to the Commission to make submissions on the point, the
> determination of the President under Article 28 of Legislative Decree 25/2008 cannot at present be said to exist, given the absence of a signature on the document
> in question.

The consequence is drawn from the principle of Joint Chambers No. 11399/2024, recalled at length: the correctness of the accelerated
procedure must be assessed «not only with regard to compliance with the time limits of the administrative
procedure, but also with regard to compliance with the various stages into which the
procedure itself is divided». The characterisation of the procedure as accelerated must stand «as the logical
antecedent of the final decision, and cannot derive from the mere formula of "manifest unfoundedness"
contained in the decision of rejection».

## The reference to Court of Cassation No. 12444/2025

The decree anchors the point in Cass. No. 12444/2025, according to which the applicant «cannot be denied
the possibility of knowing in advance the procedural model under which his
application will be examined, also in order to challenge any erroneous determination by the President
of the Commission and to be notified of it when the outcome is communicated». The right to prior knowledge
presupposes that a determination exists and can be identified: an unsigned document does not
satisfy the requirement, and the defence during the administrative procedure is compromised from the outset.

## The procedural outcome

Automatic suspension under Article 35-*bis*(3) of Legislative Decree 25/2008, as the ordinary procedure applies again. The decree
is in line with the other Bologna decisions published on this site which, when the defect
concerns the stages of the special procedure (determination, time limits, conditions), apply the
principle of Joint Chambers No. 11399/2024. What is peculiar to this ruling is that it isolated a minimal defect
— the absence of a signature on a single act in the administrative procedure — as sufficient to cause the
logical antecedent of acceleration to fall. As to evidence, the burden falls on the administration:
invited to make submissions, it did not produce a signed determination, and the gap cannot be cured in judicial
proceedings.
