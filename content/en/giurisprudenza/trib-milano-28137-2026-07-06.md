The Tribunale di Milano (judge Dr Elena Masetti Zannini) refuses to validate the detention in the CPR
in via Corelli of a Tunisian national, ordered by the Questore of Padua on 2 July 2026 under Article 6(2),
points (*b*), (*c*) and (*d*), of Legislative Decree 142/2015. The application for protection had been rejected by the
Commissione of Padua on 8 July 2025 and the appeal was pending before the Tribunale di Venezia, which had
granted interim relief on 29 January 2026; an expulsion of 2021 had remained unenforced. It is the first
decision, among those collected, to apply Article 6 as rewritten by Decree-Law 100/2026 and the new
Article 5-*sexies* on the risk of absconding.

## Points (b) and (c): habitual conduct and dangerousness must be proved

The Questore based point (*b*) on the categories of Article 1 of Legislative Decree 159/2011 and point (*c*) on convictions for
narcotics offences. The Tribunal starts from Cass. no. 26173/2023: the judicial review "must concern […]
verification of the existence of the conditions for the foreigner's belonging to one of the categories",
with an objective assessment, currency and an overall examination of the personality. From the criminal
record obtained ex officio "nothing emerges"; from the fingerprint records only a single complaint of 2
August 2024, while a precedent of 2021 belongs to the brother. Hence no habitual conduct, and no
concrete and current dangerousness: convictions do not operate automatically (Const. Court no. 202/2013)
and for the one indicated by the Questore "there is no confirmation whatsoever in the criminal record".

## Point (d) after Decree-Law 100/2026

Point (*d*) remains made up of two hypotheses "linked by the conjunction 'and'": the need to determine the
elements of the application that cannot be obtained without detention and the risk of absconding, now
referred to Article 5-*sexies* of Legislative Decree 142/2015, which lists the circumstances and requires assessment "case by case". The
decree sets out recitals 19, 23, 24 and 28 and the definitions of Article 2 of Directive (EU) 2024/1346. In
the case at hand, the false statement of identity is not borne out by the records; the failure to comply
with the expulsion and the absence of a passport are to be read in the light of the individual context.
But the decisive defect is the failure to invoke the first condition: the application "was rejected on 8
July 2025, therefore almost a year before the detention", so that the provision "probably could not even
find application in the concrete case". Cass. no. 21523/2026 is cited, which holds generic an appeal that
does not indicate which elements remain to be determined, and the "only rebuttable" presumption of
absconding arising from the lack of a passport. Integration — continuous work albeit without a
contract, statements of accommodation, promise of employment — completes the picture.

The same reasons return in [Trib. Milano 33106](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-milano-33106-2026-08-03.html)
and, for the alternative measure, in [Trib. Milano 33590](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-milano-33590-2026-08-06.html).
