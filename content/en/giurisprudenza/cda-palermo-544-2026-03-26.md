The Court of Appeal of Palermo, First Civil Division, ruling on the point reserved at the
hearing held by written submissions, has granted — reversing Decree No. 2870/2026 of 16 March 2026 of the
Tribunal of Palermo — the request for **suspension of the enforceability** of the decision by which the
*Commissione territoriale* (Agrigento Section) had rejected the application for international
protection as **manifestly unfounded**, pursuant to Article 35-*bis*(4-*bis*) of Legislative Decree 25/2008.
