The Tribunale di Bologna, specialised section for immigration, international protection and free
movement of EU citizens (judge Dr Luca Minniti), granted the application for **authorisation to
remain** in the national territory for the entire duration of the proceedings, brought by an applicant
whose **subsequent application** had been declared inadmissible by the Commissione territoriale of
Bologna — Forlì-Cesena Section — on the ground that it contained no new elements.

As to the **applicable law**, the decree starts from Article 79(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348, which
ties the application of the new rules to applications lodged "from 12.6.26": since the application at
issue was lodged by means of the C3 form of 12 June 2026 — even though it had been registered in the
preceding days — the Regulation applies, contrary to what the applicant's counsel argued.

On the merits, the Tribunal finds a **conflict between the domestic provision and the Regulation**.
Article 11 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 provides that, before a decision on admissibility is taken pursuant to
Article 38, the applicant must be given the opportunity to have an *admissibility interview*, in which
to put forward the reasons why the grounds of inadmissibility do not apply to his or her case; Article
55(4) allows the personal interview to be dispensed with **only** where it is clear from the written
submission filed that the application does not involve the new elements referred to in paragraph 3. As
no other cases of omission are provided for, the new text of Article 29 of Legislative Decree 25/2008
as amended by Decree-Law 100/2026 — in so far as it omits to provide for the obligation to hold an
interview during the preliminary examination where no submission has been filed — **conflicts with the
mandatory provision** of Article 55(3) and (4) of the Regulation.

As to the facts, no interview appears to have been held and the decision expressly states that no
submission was filed, without the determining authority — on which the burden lay — accounting for
this. The Tribunal adds a systemic observation: the decision contains **no mention of compliance with
the legal counselling obligations**, which are expressly provided for, for the decision on
admissibility too, by Article 16(2), point (b)(ii), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348.

Hence the granting of the application, "as a consequence of the automatic suspensive effect of the
appeal resulting from the unlawfulness of the special procedure followed".

**Update.** Five days later the same Tribunal, with a different judge, took up and developed the
point in [Trib. Bologna, 21 July 2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-11342-2026-07-21.html): legal
counselling is a service provided by a "person" and not by a leaflet, the Commissione must state the
name of the adviser and the date of the interview, and the burden of proving compliance with the
special procedure lies with the determining authority.
