The Tribunal of Naples (judge Cristina Correale) authorises to remain a Bangladeshi applicant whose application, made
after the entry into force of the Pact, had been decided under the accelerated procedure on the basis of a recognition
rate of 20% or lower.

## The recharacterisation of the interim request

The appellant had requested suspension of the enforceability of the measure. The Tribunal observes that the application
was made «on a date after 11.6.26» and that the proceedings are governed by Article 35-*bis* of Legislative Decree
25/2008 as amended by Decree-Law 100/2026, which «provides — as the only interim protection for applications decided
under the accelerated procedure — for authorising the applicant to remain on the territory of the State». The request is
therefore recharacterised.

## The 20% threshold and membership of a category

The Commission had proceeded under the accelerated procedure invoking Article 42 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 in the part
on countries with a recognition rate of 20% or lower. The same provision excludes the accelerated procedure where the
applicant belongs «to a category of persons whose protection needs make it impossible to regard as representative» that
percentage. The appellant had alleged, already at the audition, that he had been targeted after the student protests of
July 2024 for having refused to join the demonstrators.

> considering also, in this regard, that the CT, in finding the statements on the political grounds put forward by the
> applicant not credible, in fact carried out an examination of the merits of the application, implicitly holding that
> the appellant had alleged grounds for considering in the abstract that he belongs to a category of persons whose
> protection needs make it impossible to regard as representative a recognition rate of 20% or lower, as provided for by
> the ordinary and not the accelerated procedure.

The accelerated procedure for the 20% threshold therefore presupposes that *in the abstract* there are no individual
protection needs: if the Commission enters into the merits of the credibility of an account of political persecution, it
has already acknowledged that in the abstract those needs existed, and should have examined them in the ordinary
procedure.

The decree belongs to the line of case-law that denies the Eurostat datum any automatism:
[Trib. Palermo, R.G. 9739](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-9739-2026-07-25.html),
[Trib. Torino, 31 July 2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-torino-14577-2026-07-31.html) and
[Trib. Bologna, 6 August 2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-12580-2026-08-06.html).

## Special protection as an autonomous ground

The Tribunal adds the *fumus* of the alternative claim for special protection, for the risk that return would expose the
appellant to «a condition of deprivation of fundamental rights, due to the lack of the minimum conditions to be able to
meet the unavoidable needs and requirements of personal life», citing Court of Cassation, Joint Chambers, no. 24413/2021
and Court of Cassation no. 11110/2019. Six of the eight pages are devoted to COI sources on food insecurity, poverty and
climate disasters in Bangladesh (IPC, WFP, ACCORD, IFRC, USAID), with specific attention to the district of origin: even
in interim proceedings on the authorisation to remain the *fumus* can therefore rest on the alternative claim.
