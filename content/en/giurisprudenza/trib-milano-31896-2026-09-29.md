The Tribunal of Milan (judge Francesca Minieri) holds that the legislation preceding Decree-Law 100/2026
applies to the present case and grants the interim request, suspending the enforceability
of the manifest-unfoundedness measure issued by the Commissione territoriale of
Milan. The appellant, an Egyptian national, had expressed the wish to make an
application on 17 February 2026 (passport withdrawal record, doc. 5 of the appeal), but the Questura
had arranged for its lodging only on 22 June 2026, ten days after the entry into force of
Decree-Law 100/2026. The Commission had followed the accelerated procedure under Article 42(1), points (*e*) and (*j*),
of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 and declared the application manifestly unfounded on 1 July 2026.

## Lodging is not a criterion for selecting the applicable rules

Article 79(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 refers to “applications lodged” from
12 June 2026; Article 17(4) of Decree-Law 100/2026 speaks of applications “submitted”. The Tribunal
reads the literal wording in the light of Article 26(1) of the Regulation — the application “shall be deemed to have been made
when the third-country national expresses in person the wish to receive protection” — and of
recital 27, according to which the expression of the wish “constitutes the first act that activates the application
of the Common European Asylum System”. It follows from the combined provisions that the “delay” between
the expression of the wish and lodging, attributable to the organisation of the Questure and “outside the
control” of the applicant, cannot be held against him *in malam partem*: a different reading
would turn the literal criterion into “a substantive violation of the principles of legal certainty,
protection of legitimate expectations and good administration”. For the purposes of intertemporal law alone, the expression “lodging” is to be
understood as including the moment of the expression of the wish.

## Proof of the date: the passport withdrawal record

The expression of the wish on 17 February 2026 is attested, with “certain date”, by the record of withdrawal of the
passport drawn up by the Questura: the Commissione territoriale did not file defence notes within the
time limit of Article 35-*bis*(4), and the burden of documenting a correctly
initiated accelerated procedure lies with the determining authority.

## The former regime and disapplication of the designation of Egypt

With the rules preceding Decree-Law 100/2026 applied, the principle of the Joint Chambers (Sezioni Unite)
no. 11399/2024 comes into play: the non-automaticity of the suspensive effect operates only if the accelerated
procedure was correctly adopted. The CJEU judgment of 4 October 2024, C-406/22 [*CV*] — with the
continuity given by the Grand Chamber of 1 August 2025, joined cases C-758/24 and C-759/24 [*Alace and
Canpelli*] — requires the designation of a country as safe to be disapplied where certain parts
of the territory or certain categories of persons do not satisfy the substantive conditions of Annex
I to Directive 2013/32. In the light of the MAECI country sheet of 2024 and of the
ministerial report of 1 April 2025 — which record in Egypt the continued application of the death
penalty, the weight of criticism for human rights violations and the repression of political
dissent — the Tribunal disapplies Article 2-*bis*(1) of Legislative Decree 25/2008 in so far as it
designates Egypt, with the consequent reinstatement of the ordinary procedure and suspension *ex lege*
of the contested act.

The decree forms part of the line of transitional regime case law and is in continuity with the reading of
[Trib. Bologna 13590 (Baraldi)](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-13590-2026-09-04.html) and
[Trib. Trieste 3940 (D'Alessio)](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-trieste-3940-2026-07-31.html): the
“expression of the wish” is the act that activates the Common European Asylum System and anchors the applicable
rules.
