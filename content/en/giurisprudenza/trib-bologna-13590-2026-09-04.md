The Tribunale di Bologna (judge Dr Angela Baraldi) declared automatically suspended the refusal issued by
the Commissione territoriale of Bologna — Forlì-Cesena Section. The facts come down to three dates:
expression of the wish to apply — that is, entry into reception — on 18 May 2026, C3 on 24 June, decision
of the Commissione on 27 July, taken applying Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 and declaring the application
manifestly unfounded under Article 39(4).

## "Making" and "lodging" in Article 17 of Decree-Law 100/2026

The pivot is a comparison internal to the Italian transitional provision: paragraph 2 governs the moment
at which the application is to be considered **lodged**, whereas paragraph 4 makes the preservation of
the former rules conditional on the application having been **made** before 12 June 2026. The use of two
different words in the same provision rules out that they are synonyms. Hence the rule:

> for the purposes of the transitional rules referred to in Article 17(4) of Decree-Law 100/2026, the
> application must be regarded as made before 12 June 2026 where, before that date, the applicant
> expressed the wish to apply for international protection and the subsequent lodging took place
> **late for a reason not attributable to him or her**, but traceable to the failure to comply in good
> time with the obligations incumbent on the administration.

The condition delimits the argument: the expression of the wish does not always prevail, but only where
the lodging exceeded the time limits of the former rules — three working days, six for border
procedures, extendable by ten in the case of a large number of applications, a circumstance that must be
made explicit. If the lodging took place within the time limits, the argument is not available.

## The relationship with Article 79 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348

The decree expressly rules out derogating from the Regulation: the conclusion "does not amount to a
disapplication of or a derogation from Article 79(3) […]; it concerns, rather, the identification,
under the **national transitional rules**, of the moment at which the application is to be considered
made". The two provisions operate on distinct levels: Article 79 identifies when the Regulation applies,
Article 17 of Decree-Law 100/2026 which proceedings remain under the former regime. In support, reference is
made to Court of Justice, 30 June 2022, C-72/22 PPU, on the principle of effectiveness of access to the
procedure.

## Proof of the date of the expression of the wish

Placement in reception "constitutes a **significant documentary element of the expression of the wish
having already occurred** […], given that, under Article 2 of the same decree, the status of applicant
arose also from the expression of the wish to apply for protection" ([Article 2(1)(a) of Legislative
Decree 142/2015](/patto-interattivo/dlgs-142-2015/index.html#art_2)). As regards evidence, therefore,
the prefectural notice of placement in the CAS and the accommodation statement of the managing body are
relevant.

## The outcome

Once the former rules are applied, Cass., Sez. Un., no. 11399/2024 operates: the derogation from
automatic suspension presupposes that the accelerated procedure was strictly observed in all its
stages, not only in the nine days between transmission of the application and the decision but also in
the stage preceding lodging. In the case at hand it is irregular in both, with the consequence that the
procedure is ordinary in nature, automatic suspension *ex lege* and restoration of the thirty-day time
limit for appeal. On this point the decree departs from
[Perugia](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-perugia-3003-2026-08-17.html), for which "compliance with the overall
time limit of 9 days" suffices; on this line see also
[Naples](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-napoli-17179-2026-08-04.html),
[Bologna, 12 August](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-12435-2026-08-12.html) and
[Florence](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-firenze-11120-2026-08-24.html).
