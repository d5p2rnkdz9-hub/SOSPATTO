The Tribunale di Torino (judge Fabrizio Alessandria) authorised the appellant to remain in the
territory of the State "pending the outcome of the appeal, pursuant to Article 68(2) of Regulation
(EU) 2024/1348". The decision is little more than a page long and the *ratio decidendi* lies in two passages.

## The Pact applies, but the Commissione used a repealed provision

> "noted that, although the application was lodged on a date after 12.6.2026, the PA applied the
> rules on accelerated procedures preceding the entry into force of the EU Pact on Migration and
> Asylum (Article 28-*bis* of Legislative Decree 25/2008), which are to be deemed repealed by the
> entry into force of the Procedures Regulation and of Decree-Law 100/26".

It is the mirror image of the defect found elsewhere: in
[Naples](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-napoli-17179-2026-08-04.html) and in
[Bologna](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-12435-2026-08-12.html) the Administration applies the Pact to
applications that arose before 12 June, here it applies the former rules to an application lodged after.
In both directions the error in identifying the applicable regime overturns the accelerated
procedure. According to the defence, the provision actually invoked was Article 28-*bis*(2)(c) of Legislative Decree 25/2008
— origin from a safe country of origin — whereas the decree cites
Article 28-*bis* without specifying the paragraph.

## The consequence for suspensive effect

> "held that, consequently, the exceptions to the suspensive effect of the appeal, exhaustively
> provided for by Article 68 of the Procedures Regulation, cannot apply, none of the situations
> contemplated by Article 68(3) cited above being at issue"

The reasoning reverses the burden of argument: the derogations of Article 68(3) are exhaustive and
presuppose a validly initiated accelerated procedure; once the title that supported it has fallen away,
there is no derogation to apply and the rule of paragraph 2 is restored. Hence the form of the operative
part: not a discretionary suspension, but authorisation to remain pursuant to Article 68(2).

The decree records that the application had been timely made with the appeal and that the Ministry
filed no defence submissions within three days. It is issued by the assigned judge "solely for the
purposes of the decision on suspension", with the file referred back to the presiding judge for the
setting of the hearing.
