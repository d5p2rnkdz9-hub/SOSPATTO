> ⚠️ The [Ministerial Decree of 21 July 2026](/circolari/dm-zone-frontiera-2026-07-21.html) on the border zones was
> published in the Official Gazette on 10 August 2026, six days after this decision. On the point see
> [Trib. Trieste R.G. 4748/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-trieste-4748-2026-08-20.html), according to which the ministerial
> decree must exist at the time the procedure is started.

Decree under Article 35-*ter*(4) of Legislative Decree 25/2008 of the Tribunal of Trieste (judge Marco
Brandolin): three pages that rule out the applicability of the border procedure for lack of the
ministerial decree on the border zones and draw from this the conversion of the procedure. The contested refusal
had been adopted by the Commissione territoriale of Trieste on 14 July 2026 pursuant to Article
28-*ter* of Legislative Decree 25/2008, in the accelerated border procedure under Article 42(1), point (*j*), and Article 45(1) of Regulation (EU)
2024/1348.

## The absence of the ministerial decree on the border zones

Article 45(1) refers to Article 43(1), which allows the border procedure to be started only for
an application made at an external border crossing point or in a transit zone; and Article 28-*bis* of Legislative Decree
25/2008, in the text resulting from Decree-Law 100/2026, leaves it to a decree of the Minister of the Interior to
identify those zones.

> “although Article 28-*bis* clearly states that the conduct of the procedure in question requires
> the issuing of a decree of the Ministry of the Interior identifying the border and transit zones,
> **that source has not yet been adopted**.”

It follows that the conditions of Article 43(1), point (*a*), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 were not met and that the effects produced
by the procedure must be eliminated “already in today's interim phase”. In the same sense, in proceedings on a complaint under Article
5-*quinquies* of Legislative Decree 142/2015, the Trieste decrees of
[7 August](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-trieste-4412-2026-08-07.html) and of
[8 August](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-trieste-4538-2026-08-08.html).

## The conversion of the procedure and the automatic suspension

The prerequisite having fallen away, “the procedure followed in the present proceedings must be changed, Article 35-*bis* of Legislative Decree
25/2008 and not Article 35-*ter* being applicable”; paragraph 3 of the former therefore applies,
and the disapplication of the border procedure excludes the exception of Article 68(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348,
“with the consequent re-expansion of the general rule of automatic suspension”. The operative part records
that the conditions are not met,
orders the conversion of the procedure and declares the contested measure automatically suspended for the whole
duration of the proceedings.

## The decree of the Justice of the Peace in the same case

The day before, the Justice of the Peace of Trieste (judge Francesco Benincampi, R.G. 1738/2026) had revoked
the measure alternative to detention applied under Article 14(1-*bis*)(*a*) of Legislative Decree 286/1998 —
[PDF](/allegati/giurisprudenza/gdp-trieste-1738-2026-08-03.pdf). While the request for authorisation to
remain is pending, the prohibition of removal applies, so that the Questore “erroneously considered the obligation to return
enforceable”; and, that prerequisite being lacking, “the requirements for the application of the
measures alternative to detention at the C.P.R. are not met either”. This is the same
order of verification affirmed on the same date by
[Cass. no. 24430/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/cass-24430-2026-08-04.html).

> and (4) are attributed by oversight to “Legislative Decree 286/98” instead of Legislative Decree 25/2008. The appellant
> is a Colombian national, a potential victim of trafficking and suffering from diabetes: information reported by the defence and
> not inferable from the text of the decisions.
