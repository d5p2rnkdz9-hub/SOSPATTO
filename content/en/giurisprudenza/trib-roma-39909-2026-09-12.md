The Tribunale di Roma (judge Dr Francesca Giacomini), setting the written-procedure hearing for
discussion on 23 December 2027, authorises a Georgian national to remain until the outcome of the
proceedings and orders the immediate issue of the document under Article 4(4) of Legislative Decree 142/2015. The Commissione
had applied accelerated examination under point (*j*) of Article 42(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 (recognition rate for
Georgia equal to or below 20%), also indicating points (*d*) and (*h*);
the Administration filed no submissions within the time limit of Article 35-*bis*(4) of Legislative Decree 25/2008.

## The ex officio verification also covers the guarantees of Chapter II

The decree starts from SS.UU. no. 11399/2024: the judge verifies of his or her own motion the correctness
of the special procedures, "both as regards the occurrence of the conditions laid down by Article 42 […]
and as regards compliance with the fundamental principles and procedural guarantees imposed by Chapter
II". Without defence submissions, the scrutiny is carried out on the contested decision alone: it shows
no information on the procedure and on rights (Article 8(2)-(6) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348), the presidential order is
referred to but not filed, there is no mention of the information on free legal counselling due
"at the latest upon registration" (Article 15(2)), and there is no assessment of special procedural
guarantees (Articles 20 and 21). The statement that the applicant is not in relevant health conditions
under Article 19 of the Consolidated Immigration Act (Legislative Decree 286/1998) is not equivalent to that assessment; and Article 21(2) is referred to by Article 42(1) "as a
priority saving clause", so that the authority must rule out the need for support incompatible with
accelerated examination before accelerating.

## The three conditions of Article 42, one by one

Point (*j*) requires the Administration to prove the statistical percentages, "as this is a constitutive
fact of the examination": the Commissione "gave no reasons on the point". Point (*d*) — application made to delay
removal — is "devoid of any reasoning whatsoever" and contradicted by the wife who lives and works
lawfully in Italy, a situation protectable under Article 8 ECHR and capable of grounding complementary
protection. Point (*h*) cannot rest on irregular stay prolonged without an application, "having regard
[…] to the notoriously complex formalities for access to the procedure" and to the long waits between
expression of the wish and lodging.

## The outcome

The absence of the conditions having been established, the procedure is ordinary and the effects of the
decision are suspended under Article 68(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348; the applicant is authorised to remain (Article 68(2); Article 35-*bis*(4-*bis*) of Legislative Decree 25/2008)
with issue of the document. The decision converges with
[Trib. Bologna 11342](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-11342-2026-07-21.html) on legal counselling and
with [Trib. Napoli 17573](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-napoli-17573-2026-08-10.html) on the burden of proof of
the statistics; it diverges from [Trib. Firenze 11088](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-firenze-11088-2026-09-11.html),
of the same day, on the effect of the breach of the information obligations.
