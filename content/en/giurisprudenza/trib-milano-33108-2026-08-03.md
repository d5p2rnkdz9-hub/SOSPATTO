The Tribunal of Milan (judge Elena Masetti Zannini) did not validate the obligation to report weekly
to the Questura, imposed by the Questore of Milan under Article 6(3) of Legislative Decree
142/2015 on an Egyptian national who had been detained in the Milan-Corelli CPR and was released at the same time as
he made the application for international protection, regarded as a sham because
made «at the same time as a return operation».

## The precondition: the existence of grounds for detention

The decree starts from a systemic observation. The alternative measure of Article 6-*quater* of Legislative Decree 142/2015
presupposes that the conditions for the detention of the applicant under Article 6 of Legislative Decree
142/2015 are met «upstream»: if they are lacking, the measure has no legal basis. The reason lies in the characterisation of non-detentive
custody: «less restrictive than deprivation of liberty even though it may involve a certain
level and intensity of coercion», the difference from detention being one of degree and
intensity, not of nature (citing ECtHR, *Nolan and K. v. Russia*, 12.2.2009). The grounds for
detention are exhaustive, listed in the Reception Conditions Directive 2024/1346 and transposed
nationally.

## Whether the application is abusive: empirical criteria, not presumptions

On the assessment under Article 6(3), the Tribunal accepts the approach that, in the absence of
exhaustive statutory parameters, identifies empirical criteria: length of stay in the territory without
ever having applied for protection; established opportunities for dealing with the public administration — the only criterion
mentioned by Article 8(3), point (d), of the Reception Conditions Directive, under the heading of «opportunity to
access the asylum procedure»; previous residence permits not followed by an application; family
or kinship network; failure to express the wish at the validation hearing before the
Justice of the Peace or in the days immediately following.

In the present case the investigation shows a **discrepancy in the dates of entry**: the decree of the Prefect
of Milan — based on Questura documents — indicates 1 January 2025 from the Ventimiglia border; the
information sheet of 25 July 2026 is neither translated nor signed; the person concerned, at the validation hearing,
states that he has been in Italy «for about twenty days» from Portugal; a second information sheet, of 29
July, translated and signed, gives 5 July 2026 as the date of entry by air. None of the
sheets records the notice of the possibility of applying for protection. It must therefore be presumed
that the information was received only on return to the CPR after the validation of detention — «as happens in all cases». The short time elapsed and the absence of contrary elements
rule out that the application is a sham: the conditions for detention are lacking and, with them, those of the alternative
measure.
