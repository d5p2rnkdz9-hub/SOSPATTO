The Tribunal of Turin (judge Fabrizio Alessandria) authorises the Pakistani national
appellant to remain in the territory of the State pending the judgment on the merits. The Commissione
territoriale had followed the accelerated procedure under Article 42(1), point (*j*), of Regulation (EU)
2024/1348, taking as its parameter the recognition rate below 20% in the EU for
Pakistan. The application had been lodged after 12 June 2026, with the Procedures Regulation and
Decree-Law 100/2026 therefore applying.

## The statistical threshold does not absorb the merits

The decree does not enter into the question of whether Article 42(1), point (*j*) operates automatically — a matter on which
other Tribunals are divided — but it deprives it of operative significance: the statistical threshold,
as a matter of procedure, allows the examination to be accelerated, but does not bind the assessment of the need
for protection, which remains anchored to the concrete elements of the case. The inquiry conducted of the Tribunal's own motion
shows that the appellant comes from Mardan, in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, a region “currently” in a
“situation of generalised violence in constant deterioration, such as to amount to
a situation of ‘indiscriminate violence’ generated by ‘armed conflict’ within the meaning of point (c)
of Article 14 of Legislative Decree 251/2007, such as to endanger the life and physical integrity” of the
appellant if returned.

## Authorisation to remain under the new regime

The request was made in good time pursuant to Article 35-bis(4) of Legislative Decree 25/2008 and
Article 68(3) to (5) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348. The Tribunal, applying Article 68 and the
new paragraph 4-bis of Article 35-bis — introduced by Decree-Law 100/2026 — finds the “serious and
substantiated grounds” for granting the request. The decree authorises the appellant to remain, orders
notification to the Ministry (which may file its defence notes within 20 days) and sets the hearing on the
merits for 15 December 2026.

The decree belongs to the line of case law rejecting the automatism deducible from the mere statistical designation
of Article 42(1), point (*j*) — [Trib. Palermo 9739](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-9739-2026-07-25.html),
[Trib. Roma 39909](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-roma-39909-2026-09-12.html) — with a distinct approach:
not disapplication of the parameter, but its being overcome at the level of the interim merits
through the verification of the COI on the region of origin. The assessment remains open for
the final decision.
