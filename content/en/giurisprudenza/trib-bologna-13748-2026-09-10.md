The Tribunale di Bologna (judge Marco Gattuso) establishes the right to remain of an applicant who
arrived on 21 July 2026, whose application the Commissione territoriale of Bologna had rejected as
manifestly unfounded under Article 39(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 in the border procedure
(Article 42(1), point (*j*)). The decree expressly refers to Trib. Bologna, 7 September 2026 (judge
Baraldi) and develops its point: the vulnerability identified at screening and the border procedure.

## The screening does not close the assessment

The Agrigento screening form records the need for “psychological support” and classifies the
applicant as a “person subjected to other serious forms of psychological, physical or sexual violence,
person subjected to torture, inhuman and degrading treatment”; on the same form a police officer ticks
the boxes for special reception needs and special procedural needs. The presidential determination of
30 July makes no mention of this; the rejection states that the conditions were “the subject of a
specific assessment during the interview”, but the record shows a single question (“how are you
now?”) and no exploration of the six abductions in Libya, the last on 23 June 2026, or of the debt
incurred for the journey, with indicators of trafficking. There is no record that the applicant was
informed of the right to free legal counselling (Articles 8(2), point (*d*), 15 and 16 of Regulation
(EU) 2024/1348), nor of any assessment of the category for which the 20% rate is not representative.

## Reception needs and procedural needs are different things

The decisive point is the unlawful adoption of the border procedure for a vulnerable applicant. Under
Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 there is no longer an automatic link between vulnerability and
incompatibility with the special procedures, but Article 53(2), point (*c*), excludes from the border
procedure “applicants who are in need of special procedural guarantees”, and a contrary certification
by a police officer or a medical practitioner “cannot be regarded as decisive”: they may verify the
capacity to understand and to stand in proceedings, “but obviously have no specific competence as
regards the particular procedural and judicial dynamics of international protection”. Special
reception needs relate to the facility; procedural needs require “time — which cannot be compressed —
and arrangements” that ensure genuine, complete and reliable statements. An “extremely concise”
interview on torture that occurred a few days earlier confirms the incompatibility.

## The outcome

The border procedure was applied without the necessary conditions and its stages were not respected.
The principle laid down by the Joint Chambers (Sezioni Unite) of the Court of Cassation, no.
11399/2024 — the derogation from automatic suspension operates only for an accelerated procedure that
has been correctly conducted — remains applicable under the new framework: the court verifies of its
own motion and, failing that, establishes the right to remain under Article 68 of Regulation (EU)
2024/1348.
