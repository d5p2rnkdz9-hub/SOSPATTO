The Tribunal of Palermo (judge Michele Guarnotta), deciding the complaint under Article
5-*quinquies* of Legislative Decree 142/2015 of a Pakistani national subject to the border procedure (Articles
43, 45 and 54 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348), revoked the decision of the Prefect of Agrigento of 18
June 2026 that authorised him to reside exclusively at the reception centre of
Siculiana (AG) for a maximum of twelve weeks, making material reception measures conditional
on actual residence in that place (Article 5-*ter*(4) of Legislative Decree 142/2015). The same Prefect's decision
is at the origin of the complaint
[R.G. 8694/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-8694-2026-07-25.html).

By decree of 27 June 2026
([PDF](/allegati/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-8695-2026-06-27.pdf)) the enforceable effects had already
been suspended *inaudita altera parte*: Article 5-*quinquies* provides for the complaint but contemplates
no typical interim measure, whence the admissibility of atypical protection under Article 700 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Information obligations and the gap in the implementing rules

Articles 5-*ter*, 5-*quater* and 5-*quinquies* of Legislative Decree 142/2015 do not regulate the information
obligations imposed by Article 9(5) of Directive (EU) 2024/1346, and it is precisely this gap that «requires
particularly strict verification, in the individual case, that the information required by
Union law has actually been provided». The Administration did not prove that it had
informed the complainant of the concrete content of the authorisation, of the consequences of its
breach and of the features of the border procedure: the decision was drafted in
Italian, does not appear to have been translated into Urdu and bears only the words «for notification». The
«mere reference […] to the form containing a hyperlink or a QR code» is not sufficient.

## The vulnerability that emerged from the screening

The *screening* form recorded the complainant's referral to the anti-trafficking body. Article 21 of Regulation
(EU) 2024/1348 requires the accelerated procedure and the border procedure not to be applied — or to be terminated — where support for applicants in need of special procedural guarantees
cannot be effectively ensured; the presidential determination instead asserted
«apodictically» that the conditions of Articles 21(2) and 53 were absent. Since
subjection to the border procedure is the precondition for the authorisation under Article 5-*ter* of Legislative Decree 142/2015,
the unlawfulness of the former affects the Prefect's decision.

## The grounds rejected

The ground on the incompetence of the President of the *Commissione territoriale* is unfounded, as he «does not
exercise an autonomous power introduced by Article 43» but verifies the regulatory conditions.
The plea of *de facto* detention is also unfounded: citing CJEU, Grand Chamber,
*FMS*, C-924/19 PPU and C-925/19 PPU, the Tribunal finds that the obligation to return by 10 p.m.
«relates exclusively to freedom of movement», not to personal liberty under Article 13 of the
Constitution.

## The ex officio point on the Eurostat figure

Verification of the lawfulness of the border procedure is carried out ex officio. The determination «is
limited to a generic reference to the Eurostat statistical figure» without indicating its source: the figure has
only indicative value and does not replace individual assessment, in line with
[R.G. 9739/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-9739-2026-07-25.html). Costs are set off owing to the
novelty of the issues.
