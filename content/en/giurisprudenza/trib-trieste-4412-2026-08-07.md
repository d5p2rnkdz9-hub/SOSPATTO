> published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale on 10 August 2026, three days after this decision; it supplements the 2019 decree
> without replacing it, so the objection of vagueness made here retains its significance. On the moment
> by which the ministerial decree must exist see
> [Trib. Trieste R.G. 4748/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-trieste-4748-2026-08-20.html).

The Tribunale di Trieste, **holiday-period section** (judge Dr Lavinia Claudia Dinoi), suspended
*inaudita altera parte* the decision by which the Prefect of Gorizia had authorised the complainant
to reside "exclusively" at the former C.A.R.A. centre in Gradisca d'Isonzo for a maximum of twelve
weeks (Article 5-*ter*(2) of Legislative Decree 142/2015), ordering the immediate interruption of its
implementation.

## The ministerial decree of 5 August 2019 does not support the new border procedure

The ministerial decree required by the new Article 28-*bis* of Legislative Decree 25/2008 has not been adopted, and the
President of the Commissione territoriale applied the border procedure by referring to the ministerial decree of 5
August 2019, which designates as border or transit zones the entire territory of the provinces of
Trieste and Gorizia. The Tribunal disagrees for three reasons. The 2019 decree **implements a repealed provision**: it was adopted in
implementation of Article 28-*bis*(1-*quater*), then in force, to extend the accelerated procedure to the
applicant apprehended for having evaded controls — a condition that the current regulatory provision
no longer requires; it is therefore "referable to an entirely different legal framework". Moreover, in 2019
**the border procedure did not yet exist as such**. Finally the decree **confuses "border" and
"transit zone"**: it does not clarify whether those territories contain "an external border crossing point" and which
one it is, so that "the solution […] according to which the entire territory of the province
is to be considered relevant cannot be shared" — an observation all the more compelling because Article 43(1)(a) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348
makes the start of the procedure depend on the application being made at a crossing point or in a transit zone.

## The authorisation to reside and its manner of enforcement

On the *periculum* the Tribunal gives weight to what was alleged about the concrete enforcement of the measure: prohibition on
leaving the centre, absence of the badge to open the gates, impossibility of going to counsel's
office, escort by staff for the hearing. Such treatment, if confirmed on the
merits, "would be entirely disproportionate", resulting in "a compression of personal liberty
entirely comparable to detention, with undue confusion, and therefore breach, of the measures
referred to in Articles 9 and 10 of Directive (EU) 2024/1346". Article 5-*quater*(3) of Legislative Decree 142/2015 moreover indicates
breach of the authorisation to reside as a condition for ordering detention or alternative
measures: the two levels are distinct and consequential, so that the authorisation "can never be
accompanied by a regime of enforcement comparable to detention".

## The procedure

The Tribunal considers atypical interim relief under Article 700 of the Code of Civil Procedure admissible "in the absence of
typical interim instruments suited to ensuring immediate and effective protection of the right", referring to the
decrees of the Tribunals of
[Palermo](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-palermo-8694-2026-07-25.html) and
[Bologna](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-10979-2026-07-23.html); admissibility is tied to review
of the material implementation of the measure, for which no typified instruments exist. This is the point on which the
decree diverges from the [decree of 8 August](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-trieste-4538-2026-08-08.html), issued
by the same Tribunal, which recognises the *fumus* as to the absence of the ministerial decree but denies the *periculum* because the
complaint under Article 5-*quinquies* of Legislative Decree 142/2015 is the typical remedy.

The hearing for confirmation, modification or revocation — and for decision on the merits under Article 5-*quinquies*(3) of Legislative Decree 142/2015 — is
set for 17 September 2026.
