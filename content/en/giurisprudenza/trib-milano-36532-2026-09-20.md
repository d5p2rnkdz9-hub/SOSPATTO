The Tribunale di Milano (judge Elena Masetti Zannini) finds that the conditions for the accelerated
border procedure applied to a Tunisian applicant are not met, converts the procedure from Article
35-*ter* to Article 35-*bis* of Legislative Decree 25/2008 and declares automatically suspended the
rejection for manifest unfoundedness issued by the *Commissione territoriale* of Milan on 1 September
2026. The decree, issued the day after
[Trib. Milano 36319-1/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-milano-36319-2026-09-19.html), takes up its
argument on the apprehension but adds a defect of its own, concerning the sequence of the acts.

## The determination adopted before the C3 form

The applicant expressed the wish to apply at the screening on 2 August 2026 and registered the
application at the *Questura* of Cagliari on 6 August; on 27 August he was transferred to the CAS
Mancini among the migrants “pre-channelled into the PAF”. The determination of the President of the
Commissione is dated 28 August, the C3 form was completed in Milan on 31 August.

For the Tribunale, Article 28 of Legislative Decree 25/2008 must be read together with Article 42 of
Regulation (EU) 2024/1348: the choice of the accelerated procedure requires an assessment of the
application. For that reason the C3 “temporally precedes the determination”, which decides the
procedure “having assessed the elements that emerged when the primary information was collected”; the
national rules set out the preliminary phase in Articles 26-*bis* and 26-*ter* of Legislative Decree
25/2008. Here the authority applied the border procedure “without assessing the asylum application”,
and the conditions cannot be based “solely on the screening”.

## The apprehension under Article 43(1), point (b)

On the second point the decree refers to recital 57 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 and to the “close
temporal and spatial link” required by the Court of Justice (C-47/15, *Affum*, paragraph 72; Grand
Chamber, 19 March 2019, *Arib*, paragraph 46). The determination repeats the wording of the
provision; the screening form of the Questura of Cagliari gives “Monastir” as the place of notification
(5 August 2026), and the file does not show whether the applicant was rescued at sea, apprehended at
Monastir or had already been on the territory for some time.

## The consequences

With the derogation of Article 68(3), point (*a*)(ii), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 no longer
available, the automatic suspension provided for by Article 35-*bis*(3) of Legislative Decree
25/2008 revives for the whole duration of the proceedings.
