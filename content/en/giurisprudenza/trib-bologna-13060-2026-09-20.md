The Tribunale di Bologna (judge Dr Marco Gattuso) finds that the timely appeal suspended the enforceability
of the rejection as manifestly unfounded, decided by the Commissione applying Regulation (EU) 2024/1348
on the basis of the C3 form drawn up on 10 July 2026 by the Questura of Reggio Emilia. The applicant,
who had entered Italy on 28 May 2026, had sent the application to the Questura on 9 June and had
presented himself there on 10 June, receiving an invitation to return the following week.

## Proof of the expression of the wish to apply

By decree of 20 August the judge had asked the parties for a submission on the applicable regime. The
Commissione filed nothing. Since the burden of proving the correctness of the procedure lies with the
administration, which holds the records, and the principle of non-contestation applies, the expression
of the wish made before 12 June 2026 is held to be proved.

## "Lodging" in the technical sense and compatibility of Article 17(4)

The decree resolves the "apparent inconsistency" between Article 79(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348
("lodged") and Article 17(4) of Decree-Law 100/2026 ("making"). Lodging is a new institution, defined
by recital 29 and Article 28, in which the applicant, informed and with free legal counselling
(Articles 8(2), point (d), 15 and 16), sets out in person the grounds of the application; the Italian
C3 has always been its opposite, a "mere registration". The criterion of Article 79(3) therefore
concerns only lodgings that comply with the Regulation, and does not require applications made before
12 June to be lodged. The Italian legislature's choice to leave them under the former rules "does not
conflict" with the Regulation: there is no need to disapply it, "as instead held […] in some recent
first-instance precedents", nor to refer the matter to the Court of Justice. The judge stresses the
certainty of the procedure: the applicant must know whether the time limit for appeal is five, seven
or thirty days.

## The case at hand

Applying Directive 2013/32/EU and the former domestic rules, more than the seven plus two days
provided for manifest unfoundedness elapsed between the C3 (10 July) and the decision (3 August). The
procedure is not accelerated, the time limit for appeal is not halved and the appeal is timely; under
Cass., Sez. Un., no. 11399/2024 the automatic suspension is restored.

On the premise the decree conflicts with
[Trib. Bologna 14126-1/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-14126-2026-09-16.html), which holds
Article 17(4) incompatible with Article 79(3); see also
[Trib. Bologna 13590-1/2026](/en/giurisprudenza/trib-bologna-13590-2026-09-04.html).
